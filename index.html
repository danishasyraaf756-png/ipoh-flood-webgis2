📐 GIS Spatial Analysis Parameters

⛰️ Digital Elevation Model (DEM)

Evaluation of elevation contours & terrain slope to identify water runoff pathways and high-risk flood zones.

🌧️ Hydrology & River Proximity

Buffer zone analysis based on distance radius to major rivers integrated with monthly rainfall distribution.

🏢 Land Use & Drainage Infrastructure

Mapping of populated area density, impervious surface cover, and urban drainage infrastructure capacity.
